Coaching Guidelines

Specialist Feedback Checklist

Use these criteria during Phase 2 to give precise, actionable feedback.

🧠 Student B: Content & Structure

  • Hook: Did the pitch start with a compelling question or stat?
  • Clarity: Is the problem & solution immediately obvious?
  • Value: Is the main benefit clearly stated?
  • CTA: Was there a clear closing request or next step?

⏱️ Student C: Delivery & Mechanics

  • Pacing: Was the speech too fast or too slow?
  • Filler Words: Were there excessive "uhms", "likes", or "y'knows"?
  • Time Limit: Did the pitch hit close to the 90-second mark?
  • Energy: Was the tone confident and persuasive?
